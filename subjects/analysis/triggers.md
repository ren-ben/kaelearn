# Triggers: analysis

You see this, you reach for that. Grown by folios and debriefs; the source is in brackets.

- **An inequality between squares** → pass to $|x|,|y|$; $t\mapsto t^2$ is strictly increasing on $[0,\infty)$. (UE01/2)
- **Prove $A\le B$** → write $B-A$ as a square, or a sum of squares. (UE01/2)
- **Compare sets given by inequalities** → for every $\subset$ you see, write the inequality that proves it, and a point in the difference. (UE01/3)
- **Show two sets are equal** → arbitrary $x$, chain $\Leftrightarrow$ on membership, then "$x$ was arbitrary". (UE01/4)
- **A preimage of $\cup$, $\cap$ or a complement** → translate into $\vee$, $\wedge$, $\neg$. (UE01/4)
- **Is $f$ surjective?** → solve $f(a)=b$; list every condition the formula needs; check $a$ is in the domain. (UE01/5)
- **Strictly monotone** → injective; the inverse exists on the image and is monotone the same way. (UE01/6)
- **You solved an equation by $\Rightarrow$ steps** → only candidates so far: plug them back in. (UE01/7)
- **Refute an implication** → one point where the premise holds and the conclusion fails. (UE01/7)
- **An induction step for a closed formula** → factor out what the target contains. (UE01/8)
- **$|a_n-a|$ is a fraction** → one fraction; numerator up, denominator down, until it reads $\frac Cn$. (F01, Move 04)
- **A minus sign in a denominator** → keep half: $n^2-5\ge\frac{n^2}2$ for $n\ge4$. (F01, Move 04)
- **A difference of roots** → the conjugate, $\sqrt a-\sqrt b=\frac{a-b}{\sqrt a+\sqrt b}$. (F01, toolbox)
- **Supremum or infimum** → upper bound, then for every $\varepsilon$ an element beyond $s-\varepsilon$; Archimedes picks the index. (F01, Move 02)
- **A recursive sequence** → bound by induction, factor $a_{n+1}-a_n$, only then $L=g(L)$; the bound picks the root. (F01, Move 06)
- **Signs that oscillate** → split the indices by the period; two limits mean divergence. (F01, Move 05)
- **A product in a series denominator** → partial fractions, then telescope. (F01, Move 07)
- **$n!$ or $c^n$ in a series** → the ratio test with one fixed $\theta<1$; Bernoulli gives $(1+\frac1n)^n\ge2$. (F01, Move 08)
- **You need $e$ or $(1+\frac1n)^n\to e$** → not in the Skript: use $2\le(1+\frac1n)^n\le3$. (F01, toolbox)
- **An alternating series** → $\sum|a_n|$ first; then Leibniz, monotonicity by cross-multiplying. (F01, Move 09)
- **$\varepsilon$-$\delta$ with a product** → restrict $\delta\le1$, bound the other factor. (F01, Move 11)
- **A glue point or a parameter** → one-sided limits equal to the value. (F01, Move 12)
- **"Genau eine Lösung"** → IVT for existence, strict monotonicity for uniqueness. (F01, Move 14)
- **Not uniformly continuous?** → two sequences, closer and closer, where $f$ is steepest. (F01, Move 15)
- **An inverse function** → continuous, strictly monotone, image by the IVT, then the theorem. (F01, Move 16)
- **"Beweisen oder widerlegen"** → sixty seconds against the bestiary first. (F01, Part VIII)
