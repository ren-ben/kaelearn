# Triggers: physics

You see this, you reach for that. Grown by folios and debriefs; the source is in brackets.

## Rigid bodies (folio 01, German)

- **Extended body in a uniform gravitational field** → let $M\vec g$ act at the centre of mass; $\vec D = M\vec R_s\times\vec g$. (F01)
- **Equilibrium, forces at several places** → $\sum\vec F = 0$, $\sum\vec D = 0$ about the point where most unknowns act. (F01)
- **Unknown force in a hinge, bearing or contact point** → torque or angular momentum about exactly that point. (F01)
- **Rotation about a fixed axis** → $D_z = I\dot\omega$, with $I$ about exactly that axis. (F01)
- **Axis parallel to one through the centre of mass** → Steiner, $I = I_s + Md^2$, always via $S$. (F01)
- **An integral for $I$** → symmetry first: cylindrical coordinates, $I_x+I_y+I_z = 2\int r^2\,\mathrm dm$, mirror planes kill products of inertia. (F01)
- **Rolling without slipping** → $v_s = \omega R$; static friction does no work, so energy with $\frac12(1+k)Mv^2$, $k = I_s/MR^2$. (F01)
- **Rope over a pulley with mass** → different tensions on each side, $(T_2-T_1)R = I\dot\omega$, $a = \dot\omega R$. (F01)
- **Collision with a body on a bearing** → angular momentum about the bearing; momentum and energy usually not. (F01)
- **Something contracts, no external torque** → $I\omega$ constant; $E = L^2/2I$ changes. (F01)
- **Sliding turns into rolling** → angular momentum about a point on the surface; $v = v_0/(1+k)$. (F01)
- **Bearings loaded, body wobbles** → $\vec L\nparallel\vec\omega$: not a principal axis. (F01)
- **Torque perpendicular to a large $\vec L$** → precession, $\Omega = D/L$. (F01)
- **Extended body swings** → $T = 2\pi\sqrt{I_A/(Mgd)}$, $I_A$ by Steiner. (F01)
